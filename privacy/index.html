Privacy notice

Last updated 2026-11-09

Callout Web (Joshua Spiers, sole trader, UK) is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: calloutweb.hello@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you order: your name, business details, email, phone number, photos you send, and your order details. Payments are processed by our payment provider; we don't see or store your full card details.

Why

To build, publish and support your website (contract), to keep financial records (legal obligation), and to answer your enquiries (legitimate interests).

Who processes it

Email (Google), form delivery (FormSubmit), payment processing (Stripe), website hosting (GitHub Pages or Cloudflare), and AI tools (Anthropic's Claude) used to build sites and draft replies.

How long

Order and financial records for 6 years. Everything else is deleted within 12 months of the end of our service, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing calloutweb.hello@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Enquiries sent through our clients' websites

If you send a quote request through a website we host, it goes to that business. A copy also comes to us so we can count enquiries for the business's monthly report; we don't contact you or use it for anything else, and we delete it within 90 days.

Preview tool

The free preview runs in your browser. Nothing you type there, or any photo you add, is sent to us unless you press Order.
