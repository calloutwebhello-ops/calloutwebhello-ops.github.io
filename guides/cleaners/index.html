Guide · Cleaner

Website for cleaners: get regular clients, not one-off calls

Cleaning clients want to know three things: do you cover my area, what does it cost, and can I trust you in my home? A cleaning website should answer all three on the first screen.

Five things every cleaner website needs

  1. Starting prices or an hourly rate

    Cleaning is one of the few trades where customers expect a guide price. 'From £X per hour' cuts down on time-wasters.

  2. Insurance and vetting

    Say if you're insured, and if staff have DBS checks, say that too, but only if it's true.

  3. Regular, end-of-tenancy and deep cleans listed separately

    They are different searches and different customers.

  4. A booking request form

    Ask for postcode, property size and how often they want a cleaner.

  5. Areas covered

    Name the towns.

What to avoid

Unrealistic promises such as 'guaranteed deposit back', which you can't control.
