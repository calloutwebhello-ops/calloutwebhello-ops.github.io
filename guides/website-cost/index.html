How much does a tradesman website cost in the UK?

Prices range from free DIY builders to £2,000+ agency sites. For most one- to three-person trade firms, a simple, fast, one-page site with a call button and a quote form is enough, and it shouldn't cost more than a few hundred pounds.

OptionTypical costTrade-offs
Free DIY trade website builders£0, or around £100/year for a custom domainYou build it yourself. Free versions usually show the provider's branding and an address on their domain.
DIY website builder (Wix, Squarespace etc.)£0–£25/monthYou do it yourself. Monthly fees add up, and it takes time to make it look good.

Prices are typical ranges seen on UK providers' websites and reviews, November 2026. Check current prices with each provider. Sources: Expert Market: Yell website review · Websites 4 Trades pricing · Powered Now: Here Is My Work

Things to check before you pay anyone

  1. Your domain name (e.g. yourbusiness.co.uk) usually costs around £10 a year, and it should always be registered in your name.

  2. Hosting for a simple static site can be free on services like Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages.

  3. Ask any provider: who owns the domain, can I take the site if I leave, and is there a minimum term?
