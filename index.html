Questions

Common questions

Is the preview really free?

Yes. The preview builds in your browser as you type. You only pay if you order.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No. Starter and Pro are monthly with no setup fee. Cancel any time by email and you won't be charged again.

Do I own the website?

Your domain is always registered in your name. On Own it you own the site outright. On a monthly plan, if you cancel we send you the site files so you can host them anywhere.

Who builds it?

Callout Web uses AI tools to build and look after sites, and every order is checked against the details you supply. We never add credentials or claims you haven't given us.

How fast is it?

Usually live within one working day of payment. Connecting your own domain can take up to 48 hours.

Where do you work?

Anywhere in the UK. Everything happens online, so it doesn't matter where you're based.

How do I know it's working?

On Starter and Pro you get a short monthly email showing how many quote requests came through your site.

Will it get me to the top of Google?

No one can honestly promise that. We build the site so Google can read it and link it to your Google Business Profile. Rankings depend on your area, competition and reviews.

What if I don't like it?

You see the full design before paying. If we can't put it right within 14 days of payment, we refund you.
