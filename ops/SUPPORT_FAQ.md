# Support answers (used by the AI operator)

Reply within the daily run. Short, friendly, plain English. Sign off "Callout Web".

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How long until it's live? | Usually the next working day after payment. Your own domain can take up to 48 hours after DNS changes. |
| Can I change something? | Yes. Reply with exactly what to change. On Starter and Pro, small edits are included any time. On Own it, two rounds in the first 30 days, then £25 each. |
| My form isn't sending | FormSubmit sends a one-time activation email to your address. Search your inbox (and spam) for "FormSubmit" and click the link. |
| Do I own it? | Yes. The domain is in your name and you can have the files at any time. |
| How do I cancel? | Reply "cancel". Your plan stops at the end of the month you've paid for and you won't be charged again. Want your site files? Just ask. (Operator: ESCALATE so the owner cancels in Stripe.) |
| Can you get me to the top of Google? | We can't promise rankings. Your site is set up so Google can read it. Linking it from your Google Business Profile and collecting reviews helps most. |
| Can I have more pages? | Pro (£39/month) includes up to 5 extra service or town pages. Want to switch? (Operator: ESCALATE plan changes; the owner updates Stripe.) |
| I want a refund | ESCALATE. Reply: "Thanks for letting me know. I've passed this to the owner, who'll reply within one working day." |
| Do you do Facebook / ads / logos? | Not at the moment. We only do websites for tradespeople. |
| Is this done by AI? | Yes. We use AI tools to build sites and handle email, and every site is checked against the details you supplied. |
| How many enquiries am I getting? | On Starter and Pro you get a monthly email with the count of quote requests from your site. |
| Do you cover my area? | Yes, anywhere in the UK. Everything is done online. |
